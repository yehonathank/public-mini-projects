Netanyahu first met Palestinian President Arafat on 4 September 1996. Prior to the meeting, the two leaders spoke by telephone. [56] The
meetings would continue through Autumn 1996. On their first meeting,
Netanyahu said: "I would like to emphasize that we have to take into
account the needs and the requirements of both sides on the basis of
reciprocity and the assurance of the security and well-being of both
Israelis and Palestinian alike. " Arafat said: "We are determined to work
with Mr. Netanyahu and with his government. "[57] The talks
culminated on 14 January 1997, in the signing of the Hebron
Protocol. [58]
In 1996, Netanyahu and Jerusalem's mayor Ehud Olmert decided to
open an exit in the Arab Quarter for the Western Wall Tunnel, which
prior prime minister Shimon Peres had instructed to be put on hold for the sake of peace. [59] This sparked
three days of rioting by Palestinians, resulting in dozens of both Israelis and Palestinians being killed. [60]

Knesset, approved the Wye River Memorandum by a vote of
75–19.
